Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how FreshBread Publishing ("FreshBread", "we", "us"), a subsidiary of Lehem Network, collects and uses personal information when you visit apps.lehemnetwork.org, buy or use our WordPress plugins, use our apps (Earshelf and Tapestry), or use ServiceLock. For privacy questions, contact us at yomi@lehemnetwork.org.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact and support: your name, email address, and message when you use the contact form or email us.
  • Purchases: when you buy a paid plan, our Merchant of Record, Creem, collects your name, email, billing details, country, and payment information, and gives us your order and subscription details and license information. We do not receive or store your full card number.
  • App accounts: for Earshelf and Tapestry, your email address and login credentials, and the content you create or import, such as documents, notes, highlights, bookmarks, wells, and preferences.
  • Provider accounts (ServiceLock): business and contact details, service area, availability, and pricing that providers give us to run bookings.

Information collected automatically

  • Website logs: our hosting provider records technical data such as IP address, browser type, pages requested, and time, for security and to operate the Site.
  • Usage data: plan usage counters (for example, AI requests used this month), listening time per day in our apps, and license status.
  • Error reports: where enabled, error-monitoring tools receive crash details with emails, access tokens, keys, and document text removed.

Cookies and similar technologies. This Site does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Our apps store your session and settings in your browser's storage so you stay signed in and keep your preferences, and Earshelf keeps an offline copy of your library on your device. Creem's checkout may set its own cookies. The Site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads.

3. How we use information

  • To provide, secure, and maintain the Services, and to deliver the plan and license you bought.
  • To process orders, renewals, and refunds (through Creem) and to send receipts and account notices.
  • To answer support requests and contact-form messages.
  • To detect abuse, fraud, and breaches of our Acceptable Use & AI Content Policy.
  • To improve reliability and features, using aggregated or de-identified usage information where possible.
  • To meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

4. AI processing

When you use an AI feature, the content needed for that request (for example, a document passage, a review, an image, or a search question) is sent to an AI provider such as OpenAI or Google, or to a speech provider, to generate the result. These providers process the data on our behalf or on yours under their own terms. We send only what the feature needs. If you give a plugin your own API key, the key is stored in your own WordPress site, and your content goes to that provider under your account. Please do not submit sensitive personal information to AI features unless you need to.

5. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for advertising. We share it with service providers who help us run the Services, each limited to its function:

  • Creem (Merchant of Record): payments, taxes, invoicing, and subscription management.
  • Netlify and GitHub: website hosting, form submissions, and code and file hosting.
  • Supabase and our cloud server provider: accounts, databases, file storage, and the servers that run our apps and API.
  • AI and speech providers: as described in section 4.
  • Error-monitoring and email providers: to detect problems and send messages you request or need.
  • Authorities and advisers: where the law requires, to protect rights and safety, or in a business transfer, with appropriate safeguards.

6. Legal bases (EEA, UK, and similar regions)

We process personal data to perform our contract with you, for our legitimate interests (running, securing, and improving the Services), to meet legal obligations, and, where needed, with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

7. How long we keep information

  • Contact and support messages: up to 24 months after our last correspondence.
  • App accounts and content: until you delete them. Each app has a "delete all my content" option that permanently removes your content.
  • Order, license, and billing records: as long as needed for accounting, tax, and legal purposes.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. Residents of California and other US states may also ask what we collect and have it deleted; we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context advertising. To exercise a right, email yomi@lehemnetwork.org. We may need to verify your identity and will respond within one month. You can also complain to your local data-protection authority.

9. International transfers

Our providers operate in several countries, so your information may be processed outside the country where you live. Where required, we rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or adequacy decisions.

10. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls, and database rules so that each app user can read only their own content. No system is perfectly secure, so please use strong, unique passwords and tell us if you notice a problem.

11. Children

The Services are not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Third-party links

The Site may link to other websites and to WordPress.org. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will post the new version here with a new date. For material changes, we will notify paying customers by email.
