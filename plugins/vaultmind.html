VaultMind AI

VaultMind AI reads your PDFs and files, personalizes every download with the requester's name, and only hands over the link once a visitor is qualified — so gated content stops being a guessing game.

See pricing Ask a question

How it works

Reads and summarizes every file you upload, then personalizes and watermarks each download for the person requesting it. Visitors are scored before they get the link.

01

Understands what you upload

Every file dropped into your media library gets read, summarized, and tagged automatically in the background, without slowing down your upload screen.

02

Personalizes every download

Each PDF is stamped with the requester's name and a short summary before it's sent, and watermarked so it can be traced back if it's shared.

03

Qualifies before it hands over the link

Visitors answer two quick questions pulled from the document itself. Strong leads get the file immediately; softer leads confirm by email first.
