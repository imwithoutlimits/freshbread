How it works

Scans your articles for unlinked brand or product mentions, inserts cloaked tracked links automatically, and watches your existing affiliate links for breakage.

01

Finds what should be linked

Each post is scanned for brand or product mentions that match commercial intent but don't have a link yet.

02

Inserts cloaked, trackable links

Up to three keywords per article are replaced with a cloaked redirect link — never inside headings, existing links, or image alt text.

03

Watches for broken destinations

Every affiliate URL is checked daily; anything that 404s or redirects to an expired product page gets flagged before it costs you revenue.
