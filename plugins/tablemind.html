How it works

Generates formatted tables from plain text or a URL, keeps WooCommerce product spec tables in sync automatically, and collapses wide tables into mobile-friendly accordions.

01

Builds tables from plain text

Paste specs or notes in and get a structured, styled table out, ready to drop anywhere with a shortcode.

02

Keeps WooCommerce specs in sync

Product spec tables update automatically as the underlying product data changes — no manual re-editing.

03

Adapts to mobile automatically

Wide tables collapse into a tappable accordion layout on small screens instead of forcing visitors to scroll sideways.
