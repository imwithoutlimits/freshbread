CouponMind AI

CouponMind AI watches for a shopper about to abandon their cart, sizes a discount to your margin rules instead of a flat blanket percentage, and applies a single-use coupon automatically before they leave.

See pricing Ask a question

How it works

Detects when a shopper is about to leave with items in their cart, calculates a margin-safe one-time discount, and applies it automatically at checkout.

01

Catches shoppers before they leave

Exit-intent cursor movement on desktop or fast back-navigation on mobile triggers the engine only when there's an active cart.

02

Calculates a margin-safe discount

The offer is sized to the cart's value and your margin protection rules, not a single flat percentage for everyone.

03

Applies itself at checkout

A single-use, self-expiring coupon is generated and auto-applied to checkout, then cleaned up automatically once it expires unused.
