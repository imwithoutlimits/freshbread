LinkHealer AI

LinkHealer AI checks your site's links on a rolling schedule, repairs the ones that go dead, and never touches an affiliate link without your say-so.

See pricing Ask a question

How it works

Quietly checks every link on your site in the background and repairs the ones that break — pulling replacements from the Wayback Machine and flagging affiliate links for your review.

01

Checks links without slowing your site down

Scans run in small background batches, so visitors never wait on a link check and your site never feels the load.

02

Repairs what it safely can

Internal links that 404 get matched back to the right page automatically. External links that vanish get replaced with the closest archived version.

03

Never auto-edits an affiliate link

Anything that looks like an affiliate or tracked link is flagged for manual review instead of being changed automatically — your commissions stay protected.
