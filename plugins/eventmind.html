How it works

Upload a photo of an event flyer and EventMind AI reads the date, time, and venue for you, fills in the listing, and keeps a live calendar synced for your visitors.

01

Reads the flyer for you

Drop in a photo of a flyer and the event title, date, time, and venue are extracted and filled into the listing automatically.

02

Publishes calendar-ready events

Every event goes out with the structured markup search engines expect, so it's eligible to show up directly in event search results.

03

Keeps attendees synced

Visitors add events to Google Calendar or download an .ics file in one click, and stay synced if a date or time changes later.
