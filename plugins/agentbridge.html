How it works

Exposes your WooCommerce store to AI agents — ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, and Meta AI — so they can browse your catalog and check out on a customer's behalf, over REST and the emerging agent-commerce standards.

01

Free: makes your store agent-readable

The free AgentBridge AI plugin exposes your WooCommerce catalog to AI agents over a documented REST API, with the discovery endpoints agent platforms look for.

02

Pro: turns agent visits into real, paid orders

AgentBridge AI Pro adds a working Stripe-powered checkout and OpenAI Instant Checkout (ACP) support, so an agent can complete an actual purchase, not just a pending order.

03

Pro: shows you what agents are doing on your store

A dashboard logs every agent request — traffic and revenue by agent platform — plus AI-visibility monitoring that checks whether AI answers are citing your store at all.
