RecipeMind AI

How it works

Extracts recipes from text or photos, generates the SEO schema markup Google requires for rich results, and calculates nutrition automatically.

01

Reads recipes from photos

Snap a handwritten card or an old photo and the title, prep and cook times, servings, and full ingredient/instruction list get extracted automatically.

02

Publishes with valid recipe schema

Every recipe outputs Google-compliant Schema.org/Recipe JSON-LD automatically, so it's eligible for rich results in search.

03

Lets readers scale and swap ingredients

A front-end serving-size scaler recalculates quantities live, and dietary-swap suggestions (gluten-free, vegan) are generated on request.
