What Tapestry does

Find, collect, listen to, and reflect on Scripture.

01

Find the passage you need

Search by Bible reference or by topic, or start from a set of starter wells. On paid plans, AI-assisted search and recommendations choose and order passages from a prepared list of candidates. They never write Scripture themselves.

02

Collect passages into wells

A well is a collection built around a theme, a season, or a question. Add passages, keep notes and bookmarks, and let AI suggest a name for a well when you want one. Each AI suggestion is labelled so you always know what is yours and what is suggested.

03

Listen, memorize, and reflect

Listen to a passage, practise it with memorization tools, and answer reflection questions that are labelled as AI-generated prompts. A listening thread, a weekly target, and a rest day each week keep the rhythm calm: no confetti, no red warnings, no guilt.

04

Scripture is shown as stored

Passages are displayed exactly as stored, from translations whose licenses have been reviewed, beginning with the public-domain World English Bible. AI is limited to choosing, ordering, and drafting questions, and every AI output is checked and labelled “Suggested by AI” or “AI-generated reflection prompt”.

AI features and content safety

Tapestry's AI never generates or alters Scripture. It selects and orders passages from a prepared list and drafts reflection questions, and every output is validated and labelled. If no AI is available, Tapestry falls back to curated content. NSFW content is prohibited and AI requests are subject to content-safety filtering. See our Acceptable Use & AI Content Policy.
