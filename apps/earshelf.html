What Earshelf does

Turn any document into a library you can listen to and search.

01

Bring in almost anything

Import PDFs, EPUB books, Word files, Markdown, plain text, web pages and links, pasted text, and photos or scanned pages (read in your browser with OCR on paid plans). A review step lets you skip what you would rather not hear: tables, web addresses, footnotes, and back matter.

02

Listen the way you like

Choose from five reading modes and ten voice styles, set pronunciations, and follow along with sentence highlighting. Use device voices, or natural server voices on paid plans, with a sleep timer, lock-screen controls, bookmarks, notes, and offline audio downloads. Pro can export a whole document as an audiobook with a chapter per section.

03

Understand and remember

Get summaries in twelve forms and search by meaning across your library. On Pro, ask a document a question and get an answer with checked citations, or turn it into quizzes and spaced-repetition flashcards. Answers come only from your own documents, and Earshelf says so when it cannot find one.

04

Private by design

Each person's library is visible only to them. Sign in to sync across devices, export your highlights and notes (Markdown, CSV, Readwise CSV), and delete all your content from inside the app whenever you like.

AI features and content safety

Earshelf's AI features (summaries, cited answers, quizzes, flashcards, and explanations) work from the documents you import. Sexually explicit or NSFW material may not be imported or processed, and AI requests are subject to content-safety filtering. See our Acceptable Use & AI Content Policy.
