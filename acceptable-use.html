Acceptable Use & AI Content Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026.

Purpose

This policy applies to every FreshBread Publishing product: our WordPress plugins, the Earshelf and Tapestry apps, ServiceLock, and anything built on them. It forms part of our Terms of Service. Our AI features generate summaries, answers, captions, tables, recipes, audio, and similar output, so this policy sets clear limits on what may be put into them and what may come out of them.

NSFW and sexual content: not allowed

You may not use any FreshBread product to create, describe, caption, summarize, transform, store, process, or distribute:

  • sexually explicit, pornographic, or erotic content of any kind, including AI-generated text, images, or audio, and sexual or "NSFW" chatbots;
  • nudity or sexualized imagery intended to arouse;
  • any sexual content involving minors. This is absolutely prohibited. We will remove it, close the account, and report it to the relevant authorities;
  • intimate imagery of a person shared without their consent, or face-swap, deepfake, or face-manipulation content.

You also may not install or use our plugins on a website whose main purpose is to host or sell adult content, or point a plugin at an adult library (for example, asking an image-captioning plugin to describe explicit images).

Other prohibited uses

  • Illegal content or activity, or content that infringes someone else's intellectual property or privacy.
  • Hate, harassment, threats, or content that promotes violence, graphic gore, or self-harm.
  • Malware, phishing, spam, or attempts to break, overload, or bypass the limits of our Services.
  • Fake or misleading content, such as invented reviews, testimonials, or event listings, or undisclosed affiliate links where disclosure is required.
  • Scraping or republishing content you have no right to use, or collecting people's personal data without a lawful basis.
  • For ServiceLock: fake or fraudulent bookings, misrepresenting availability or prices, or abusing the deposit flow.
  • Reselling, sharing, or leaking license keys or API access.

How content filtering works

We apply content-safety filtering in layers:

  1. Provider safety systems. Every request that reaches an AI model is subject to that provider's safety and moderation systems. Requests or results they flag as sexual or otherwise unsafe are refused or returned as an error.
  2. Our handling of flagged content. When content is flagged as NSFW, our AI features refuse or discard it. It is not returned as a result, published, or saved as generated output.
  3. Product-level safeguards. In TrustMind AI, reviews flagged as spam or clearly negative stay unapproved until a person approves them. In Tapestry, AI cannot write or change Scripture, can only choose from a prepared list, and every AI output is checked and labelled. In Earshelf, AI summaries and answers are drawn only from the user's own documents and say so when nothing is found.
  4. Human review. We review reports and may review flagged accounts. Plugin owners remain responsible for what is published on their own websites.

No filter is perfect. If you see content that should have been blocked, please tell us.

Reporting

To report a violation or a product that is generating inappropriate content, email yomi@lehemnetwork.org with the subject "Content report" and as much detail as you can (page, product, and what you saw). We review reports promptly and will not penalize anyone for a good-faith report.

Enforcement

Depending on severity, we may remove or block content, pause a feature, limit an account, revoke a license or API key, or terminate access. Serious or repeated violations, including attempts to generate NSFW content, may lead to immediate termination without refund. We may report unlawful content to law enforcement.

Changes

We may update this policy as our products and the law change. The current version is always on this page.
