About

We build the tools we wished existed.

FreshBread Publishing sits at the intersection of publishing, consulting, and software — built for people who create and run content businesses.

What we do

FreshBread Publishing is the parent company behind a small suite of WordPress plugins, a digital publishing practice, and a private consulting arm. We started as a media and publishing operation and built our own automation tools out of necessity — then packaged them for other creators and businesses facing the same problems.

Software

Each plugin in our suite is scoped to solve one operational problem well: gated content and lead qualification, broken link maintenance, on-site search, event listing automation, documentation search, recipe publishing, affiliate link management, data tables, review curation, cart recovery, and image accessibility. We'd rather ship focused tools than one bloated platform.

Publishing & consulting

Alongside software, we produce digital media and written resources, and take on a limited number of private advisory engagements with creators and small teams who are building their own automated content operations.

How we work

  • Every plugin ships with a genuinely usable free tier — no fake trials.
  • We publish clear pricing and a 14-day money-back guarantee on every subscription.
  • Support goes to a real inbox, read by real people.
