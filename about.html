About

We build the tools we wished existed.

FreshBread Publishing sits at the intersection of publishing, consulting, and software — built for people who create and run content businesses.

What we do

FreshBread Publishing is a subsidiary of Lehem Network. We are the company behind a small suite of WordPress plugins, AI-agent connectors, two apps (Earshelf and Tapestry), a digital publishing practice, and a private consulting arm. We started as a media and publishing operation and built our own automation tools out of necessity — then packaged them for other creators and businesses facing the same problems.

Part of Lehem Network

FreshBread Publishing operates as a subsidiary of Lehem Network. Lehem Network is our parent organization, and this website is hosted on its domain, apps.lehemnetwork.org. Questions about the relationship between the two can be sent to yomi@lehemnetwork.org.

Software

Each plugin in our suite is scoped to solve one operational problem well, and we're extending that same focus to how AI agents interact with the businesses we build for — from letting agents shop a WooCommerce store to letting them book a real appointment with a service provider.

Apps

Earshelf turns documents into a listenable, searchable audio library. Tapestry helps people find, collect, listen to, and reflect on Scripture. Both are built and run by FreshBread Publishing.

Publishing & consulting

Alongside software, we produce digital media and written resources, and take on a limited number of private advisory engagements with creators and small teams who are building their own automated content operations.

How we work

  • The free version of every plugin is distributed on WordPress.org — install it like any other plugin.
  • The Pro version is sold directly here, with clear pricing and a 14-day money-back guarantee.
  • Support goes to a real inbox, read by real people.
