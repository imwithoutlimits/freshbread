Our apps

Two apps built by FreshBread Publishing, a subsidiary of Lehem Network: one turns your documents into audio you can listen to, the other helps you find, collect, listen to, and reflect on Scripture.

ELIVE APP
Document-to-audio library

Earshelf

Turn any document into a library you can listen to and search.

  • Import PDF, EPUB, Word, Markdown, web pages, links, and scans
  • Read with sentence highlighting, or listen with device or natural voices
  • AI summaries, cited answers, quizzes, and flashcards from your own documents

TEARLY ACCESS
Scripture listening and reflection

Tapestry

Find, collect, listen to, and reflect on Scripture.

  • Search by reference or by topic, and collect passages into wells
  • Listen, memorize, and reflect with a gentle daily thread and a weekly rest day
  • Scripture is shown as stored. AI never writes or changes it.
